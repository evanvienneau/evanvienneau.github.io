Evan Vienneau

Gravity and Astroparticle Physics

PhD Candidate at University of Alberta


I am broadly interested in exploring the physics of gravitational phenomena. I use state-of-the-art cosmological simulations to extract the phenomenology of dark matter that is relevent for to direct and indirect detection experiments. Alongside this, I make use of phenomenolgoical quantum gravity to probe the resolution of the singularity and determine the effect of quantum gravity on observables.
