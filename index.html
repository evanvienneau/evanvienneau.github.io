Gravity and Astroparticle Physics

PhD Candidate at University of Alberta

I use state-of-the-art cosmological simulations to extract the phenomenology of dark matter that is relevent for direct and indirect detection experiments. I also study black hole resolution using phenomenological theories of quantum gravity.
Feel free to check out my authored publications or view my CV. You can contact me at eviennea@ualberta.ca.

Publications

I am broadly interested in studying the physics of gravitational phenomena. I use state-of-the-art cosmological simulations to extract the phenomenology of dark matter that is relevent for direct and indirect detection experiments. Alongside this, I use phenomenological theories of quantum gravity to probe the resolution of black hole singularities and quantify it's impact on observables.
