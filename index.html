Evan Vienneau

Gravity and Astroparticle Physics

PhD Candidate at University of Alberta

I use state-of-the-art cosmological simulations to extract the phenomenology of dark matter that is relevant for direct and indirect detection experiments. I also study black hole resolution using phenomenological theories of quantum gravity.
Feel free to check out my authored publications or view my CV.
You can contact me at eviennea@ualberta.ca.
